Dear Remy,

Hi, baby. I don't really know how to start this letter, but I know that there are things I want to tell Remy properly. So, here I am, trying to put my feelings into words because Remy means so much to me.

First of all, Inas is sorry. I'm sorry for the times I become moody, for the times I overthink, and for the moments when my feelings get the better of me. Sometimes I don't know how to explain what is going on inside my head, and I end up expressing it in ways I never intended. That doesn't make it okay, and I'm sorry if I have ever made Remy feel tired, confused, or hurt.

I know that I'm not perfect. There are still so many things I need to learn about myself, about communicating, and about understanding another person's feelings. I'm trying, baby. I really am. I don't want to keep repeating the same mistakes without learning from them.

Thank you for being here with me, for the little things Remy does, and for the moments that might seem ordinary but mean a lot to me. Even the smallest things can make my day better. Sometimes I might not know how to show how grateful I am, but please don't think that I don't appreciate Remy.

Inas likes the way Remy makes me smile, the little moments we share, and the comfort of having someone I can talk to. I like Remy's smile more than I can explain. It's honestly unfair how I can be upset and then suddenly find it difficult to stay angry when Remy smiles at me.

I don't expect everything between us to be perfect. We are both human, and we can have bad days, misunderstandings, and moments when we don't know what to say. I just hope that we can keep learning to understand each other, one step at a time.

If Inas ever makes Remy feel like Remy isn't enough, please know that this is not what I want. I don't want my overthinking or my mood to make Remy question how much I appreciate having Remy in my life.

Baby, Inas cares about Remy so much. I can't promise that I'll never make mistakes again, but I can promise that I'll keep trying to communicate better, to listen, and to take responsibility when I am wrong.

I don't want to force anything or rush anything. I just want us to enjoy the moments we have and let things grow naturally. Whatever happens, I'm thankful that our paths crossed and that I got the chance to know someone like Remy.

And if Allah gives us the chance to continue growing closer, I hope we can do it with patience, kindness, honesty, and understanding.

So, once again, I'm sorry, baby. Not just because I don't want us to be upset with each other, but because Remy's feelings matter to me too. I hope this little letter can explain what I sometimes struggle to say out loud.

Please take care of yourself, okay? Eat properly, get enough rest, and don't forget that you deserve kindness too. I hope I can give Remy more good memories, more reasons to smile, and more moments that feel safe and genuine.

I love you, and I'm grateful for you. Thank you for being my favourite person to annoy and my favourite person to miss.

always with love,
Inas ♡
